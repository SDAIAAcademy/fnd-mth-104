The full wording is in course.json and the README. The oral check is scored separately (10% of the course), so nothing counts twice. Full marks on the second criterion: the interval's resampling matches the data's structure, and the notebook shows a rerun with a new seed.

The oral check · 10% of the course

Ten minutes: three on the brief, seven rerunning one test with a change

1 · The brief

The question, the estimate with its interval, the test, the limits. Three minutes.

2 · The change

The assessor names one change: a subgroup, a statistic, a number of shuffles, a seed.

3 · The rerun

You make the change, rerun the test and read the new result aloud.

Say what you expect before you run it, then compare. The reading matters as much as the code.

The change cards and what a good reading of each sounds like are in the trainer pack, not in this repository. Score the reading: effect size and interval first, p-value read correctly, and whether the change made the result more or less trustworthy (for example, a subgroup chosen now is a forking path).
